Any file that makes clinical, nutritional, or product claims requires references.

## Link Style
Use markdown reference-style links.

Example:
Here is [text][ref]. And here is [text][ref].
[ref]: https://example.com

The links go to the bottom of the document in a `References` section.

`ref`` are the citation without spaces, e.g. PMID29914147.

## Citation Format

Citations are made in the `{title} ({source} {ref_id})` format. For example:

- `Thorne, Basic B Complex, 60 Capsules (iHerb 18791)` for a supplement
- `The Effects of Thiamine on Breast Cancer Cells (PMID 29914147)` for a medical study
- `Broccoli raw (FDC 2709643)` for a food data central item
- `M-Classic Waldhonig (Migros 151401200000)` for a Migros item
- `Significant improval after biofilm removal (Reddit 1lb7n0d)` for a reddit thread
