## Safety Profile

For any supplement you recommend:
1. Research the safety profile to make sure the patient doesn't run into toxicity
2. If the patient takes multiple supplements, verify the summed amounts (e.g., total zinc from multiple sources)
3. Check for interactions with the patient's current supplements and any medications listed in their profile

## Cofactors

Taking one supplement might require other supplements. Always account for this.
Example: Vitamin D3 should always be combined with K2.
