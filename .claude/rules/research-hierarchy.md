# Research Hierarchy

For health claims, follow this evidence hierarchy:

1. **PubMed** (systematic reviews/meta-analyses > RCTs > observational studies > case studies)
2. **Reddit** — experience reports and practitioner protocols (anecdotal, not authoritative)
3. **iHerb** — product availability and formulation details


## Tools

- Use `pubmed-cli` for literature search.
- Use `reddit-cli` for user experience reports on supplements and protocols.

Always link source Reddit threads when writing content to a doc.

## Conflicting Evidence

When PubMed evidence and anecdotal reports (Reddit) conflict:
- PubMed wins for dosing, safety, and mechanism claims
- Anecdotal reports are valuable for: side effect patterns, real-world tolerability, practical tips (timing, food pairing, brands)
- Note the conflict in the document so the patient can make an informed decision
